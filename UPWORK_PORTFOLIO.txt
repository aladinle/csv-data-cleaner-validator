PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Personal demo of a Python tool that cleans and validates customer CSV exports. It normalizes whitespace and formatting, removes exact duplicate records, and flags missing values, invalid email formats, and inconsistent country codes. Deliverables include cleaned data, rejected records with reasons, an issue audit, and a standalone HTML report. The synthetic demo processes 12 records into 5 valid records and 7 flagged records. Includes 10 automated tests. Created with AI assistance; no client data used.
